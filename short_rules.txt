Video Contest — Short Rules

1. Choose a video contest you like.
2. Watch existing videos, enjoy, vote.
3. Create your own AI-generated video, drawing on the literature, history, and ideas presented for the contest.
4. Shape a mood, visual style, and narrative — make it yours.
5. Publish it.
6. Earn likes and donations — they count as votes.
7. Top creators can win rewards.

Note: submitted videos are standalone media files and are not interactive AI chat experiences.
